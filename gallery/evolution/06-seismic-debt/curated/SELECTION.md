## Artistic selection

The earthquake is no longer recorded by four aligned channels. Repeated cyan and chalk traces are displaced into stepped architectural blocks, punctuated by short orange register marks and broad black gaps. The fragmentation retains enough waveform rhythm to read as failed recording *rather than* pure digital static. The result dramatizes one event being misremembered at incompatible addresses. The original four individual voices are less distinguishable; the loss of precise seismographic anatomy is a real limitation.

The real 12-panel Windows contact sheet was inspected together with full-resolution canonical descendants **14, 24, 33, 97, 101, 102 and 125**. Rejections: #14 becomes an almost full red occlusion; #33 and #125 are visually saturated noisy fields; #97 strips the event into disconnected white dashes; #102 has disciplined, minimally disturbed horizontal signal bands; #24 is an elegant but relatively conventional clean strip arrangement. **#101** exhibits a more effective conflict between recognisable recording rhythms and spatial displacement. The diversity algorithm selected candidates for descriptor coverage, *not* aesthetic rank.
