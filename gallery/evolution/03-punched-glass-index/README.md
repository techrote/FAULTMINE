## Intent and distinction

A diagonally climbing procession of thirteen hand-coded, pseudo-glyph tablets on quiet dark-blue registration paper. Five-by-four punched bits, amber/cyan imprints, terminal wires, two missing positions, hairline carrier tracks and two void cuts suggest a writing system whose registration has been lost. The visual problem is to fracture a recognisable notational procession without making a uniform noise field.

Unlike Aperture Ossuary (#39), the source does not form twin architectural relays around a central gap. Unlike Estuary Moraine (#41), it is not a shoreline/strata study; unlike MOTH ROM (#38) it has neither chitin nor machinery. The source has no external art, font assets or third-party imagery.

The author-generated **unfaulted source BMP was actually rendered and visually inspected** at 3x nearest-neighbour enlargement. No canonical mined output is claimed visually inspected until actual Windows execution succeeds. FM-014 novelty selection is mathematical diversity, not an aesthetic approval.
