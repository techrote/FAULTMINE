## What was examined

I opened the native 4 × 3 selection contact sheet generated by Windows CI and inspected full-size selected canonical PNGs #147, #156 and #51. The panels are ordered `0, 27, 51, 55 / 82, 102, 134, 136 / 147, 156, 163, 185` (see the native contact-sheet sidecar).

**Select #147 — Punched Glass / Dislocated Registry.** The glyph tablets still climb from lower left to upper right; interrupted parallel conductors and two gaps remain legible. Cyan/magenta channel clashes outline individual tablets against a relatively uniform ochre/olive plate. The severe chromatic misregistration creates an identifiable, bounded family of marks rather than an all-over randomized interference texture. Its very flat field is a deliberate restraint, and a possible weakness for final aesthetic acceptance.

Alternatives actually examined: **#156** has an attractive near-black ground but its red/green/blue granular disintegration makes the individual stamps harder to parse; **#51** turns the panel into dense broadband raster interference and largely loses the discrete glyph procession. The contact sheet additionally shows **#27** and **#55** collapsing to near-uniform single-colour rectangles, **#163** severely bleached, **#82** dominated by fluorescent green, and **#0** a red-heavy plate. These are visual assessments of actual CPU output, not a mechanical diversity score.

Distinctiveness remains a *curatorial judgement*, not proven by descriptor novelty alone. #147 has a dedicated pixel representative and near-group representative equal to its descendant index; novelty distance 377045 exceeds the inclusive near-duplicate threshold 8192, but neither metric proves beauty.
